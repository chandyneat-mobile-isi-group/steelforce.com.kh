Privacy Policy

SteelForce is an internal business application published by ISI Steel Co., Ltd., a member of ISI GROUP (“we”, “us”). It is built for our own employees and authorised partner staff to carry out their work: managing depots, running daily visit routes, checking in at customer sites, and preparing quotations and sales orders that flow into a third-party system.

It is a work tool, not a consumer product. Accounts are issued by the company, and without a company account the app gives no access to business data. Everything the app records is work activity performed on behalf of the company, processed in the context of your employment or engagement with us.

This policy explains what data SteelForce collects, why, and who it is shared with. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the app.

Information about customers and contacts

When a representative registers a customer or records a visit, the app stores business information about that customer: company name, site location, contact name, role and business phone or email. ISI Steel is the controller of that information and uses it only to operate the sales relationship. If you are a customer contact and want to know what we hold about you, write to the address at the end of this policy.

How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and apply your access rights.
  • To run depot, route, visit, quotation and sales order features, and to sync them to a third-party system.
  • To verify that site visits took place as reported, using location and photo evidence.
  • To send operational notifications.
  • To diagnose crashes, sync failures and performance problems.
  • To protect against misuse and to meet legal, tax and audit obligations.

Location and visit evidence is used for operational verification and reporting. It is not used to track staff outside working hours; the app requests location only while a location feature is in use.

Sharing and disclosure

We never sell your data. We share it only with these types of parties:

  • ISI GROUP companies, where sales, finance or audit staff need access to visit and order records.
  • A third-party system and its hosting provider, which store and process the business records the app creates.
  • Google, for app distribution, maps and push notification delivery, as set out in the table below.
  • Professional advisers and auditors, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Public authorities, where we are required by law, court order or a valid legal request.

Storage and retention

SteelForce works offline. Your data is written to an encrypted database on your device first, then synced to our servers when a connection returns. On-device data is protected with SQLCipher encryption, and sign-in tokens are held in the Android Keystore or iOS Keychain.

Signing out wipes the SteelForce data stored on the device, and uninstalling removes the local database. Records already synced remain on the company’s systems.

Everything recorded through the app — visits, inventory counts, quotations, sales orders and customer registrations, together with the account and device data listed above — is a business record of ISI Steel Co., Ltd. These records are retained indefinitely unless the company decides to remove them.

Users cannot delete their account or request deletion of the records they create. Deletion is decided by the company as the owner and controller of the data. Questions about what is held can be sent to the contact at the end of this policy.

Security

Data in transit travels over HTTPS/TLS. Data at rest on the device sits in an encrypted database whose key is device-bound and sealed in the platform keystore; the database fails closed if the key is unavailable. Credentials and customer data are not written to logs in release builds. Access to backend records is limited to staff whose role requires it.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

Children's privacy

SteelForce is issued only to employees and authorised partner staff aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, has no features aimed at them, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

Your choices

  • Device permissions. Location, camera, microphone and notification permissions can be refused or withdrawn at any time in your device settings. Refusing one disables that feature, not the app.
  • Local data. Signing out of SteelForce erases the data held on your device.
  • Questions and corrections. If you believe a record about you is wrong, raise it with your manager or write to the contact below, and we will look into it.
  • Deletion. Records created through the app are company business records and are not deleted on request. See the Data Retention tab.

Where a mandatory law in your country gives you stronger rights than this, that law applies and we will honour it.

Data Retention

This page explains who controls the data held in the SteelForce app (com.isisteel.steelforce), how long it is kept, and what you can remove yourself.

Accounts are managed by the company

SteelForce accounts are not created by users. They are issued by ISI Steel Co., Ltd. to employees and authorised partner staff, changed when a role changes, and withdrawn when the employment or partner relationship ends. There is no self-service sign-up and no self-service account deletion.

How long data is kept

Everything recorded through the app — visits, inventory counts, quotations, sales orders, customer registrations, and the account and device data described in the Privacy Policy — forms the business records of ISI Steel Co., Ltd. These records are retained indefinitely unless the company decides to remove them, because they underpin pricing, fulfilment, commission, accounting and audit.

Users cannot request deletion of these records. Any decision to delete or anonymise them is taken by the company as owner and controller of the data.

What you can remove yourself

  1. Sign out. Signing out of SteelForce erases the encrypted database stored on your device.
  2. Uninstall. Removing the app clears anything left on the device.

Neither affects records already synced to the company's systems.

When your access ends

When you leave the company or your partner relationship ends, your access is withdrawn, your sessions are ended and the data on your device is cleared. The business records you created remain with the company.

Questions

If you want to know what the app holds about you, or believe a record is wrong, write to us and we will look into it.
